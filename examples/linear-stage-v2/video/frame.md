# Linear-stage film

The same assembly stays recognizable as its viewpoint, operating position, and component separation change.

- Canvas: 1280 by 720, 24 seconds, silent.
- Background: warm light gray `#edf0f1`; ink `#20313d`; secondary `#4b5d68`; accent `#137785`.
- Typography: Montserrat 700 for the stage title; IBM Plex Mono 400 for dimensions and timing. Both are bundled by HyperFrames. Labels are 20–24 px; headline 36 px because the geometry is the focal element.
- Focal element: the actual CAD assembly, centered below the top title band.
- Anchors: stage title at top left, stage counter at top right, the dimensional relation at bottom left.
- Supporting detail: ground contact shadows, machined metal reflections, restrained dimensional labels.
- Background: a seamless studio plane. No decorative motifs; the moving mechanism supplies the depth and motion.
